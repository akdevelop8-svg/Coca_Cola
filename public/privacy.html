Privacy Policy

How the independently operated platform collects, uses, and protects personal information.

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Operator and contact

This platform is operated by Samuel Kebede. For privacy questions or requests, contact cocacolawebsiteoperator@gmail.com.

1. Scope and brand status

This notice applies to personal information processed when people create or use an account, communicate with customer support, or use the platform's account, referral, wallet, deposit, and withdrawal features. The platform is independently operated by Samuel Kebede. It is not represented here as an official website or service of The Coca-Cola Company, and no sponsorship, endorsement, or authorization by that company is claimed.

2. Information the platform may process

  • Account details: phone number, name or display name, username, and email address where supplied; referral code and the account that referred you.
  • Authentication and security data: password hash (the password is hashed before it is stored), session/authentication tokens, sign-in and activity information, and network information such as IP address when used for rate-limiting or recording acceptance of terms.
  • Platform activity: plan selections, wallet and ledger entries, deposits, withdrawals, reward claims, check-ins, gift-code use, referral activity, and related transaction references and timestamps.
  • Payment and banking details: bank or payment method, account-holder name, account number, amount, bank transaction reference, and status or review notes. Deposit submissions may include a receipt image or PDF.
  • Support and communications: support ticket subjects and messages, replies, notifications, and information you include when contacting the operator.
  • Terms records: the version of the platform terms accepted, the time of acceptance, and the IP address recorded with that acceptance.

Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, or unrelated sensitive information in a support message.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; authenticate users; maintain referral relationships; display account and wallet activity; record and review deposits, payout-account changes, and withdrawals; prevent abuse and suspicious activity; deliver notifications; respond to support requests; and keep administrative, security, and transaction records.

4. When information may be shared

Access is limited to the operator and authorized platform administrators who need information to perform their assigned duties. The platform uses infrastructure services from Supabase for database, authentication/session-related backend operations, file storage, and server functions, and Vercel for website hosting and delivery. Information may be disclosed to a bank or payment institution when necessary to verify or complete a payment or transfer, or to a competent authority when a lawful request requires it. The operator does not offer account information for sale to advertisers.

5. Storage and security

Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords. The platform uses access controls and private file-storage access for uploaded deposit receipts. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; users should keep their passwords and account access private and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

6. Retention

Account, support, security, and transaction information is retained for as long as it is needed to operate the account and platform, preserve ledger accuracy, investigate disputes or suspected abuse, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. The platform does not currently publish a fixed deletion timetable. Some financial or audit records may need to be retained even after an account is no longer active.

7. Your requests and choices

You may contact the operator to ask what personal information is associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate account details, or ask for deletion or restriction of information. Requests will be reviewed and acted on where feasible and lawful. Information needed to maintain transaction integrity, prevent fraud, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation may not be deleted immediately. Include your registered phone number in a request only when necessary to identify the account; never include your password.

8. Cookies and session technology

The platform uses authentication/session technology to keep users signed in and protect account access. Disabling cookies or similar browser storage may prevent some account features from working correctly.

9. Children

The platform is not intended for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the operator using the email below so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when platform features or data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page. Material changes should be reviewed before continuing to use the relevant features.
